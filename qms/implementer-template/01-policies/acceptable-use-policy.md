# {{organization}}: Acceptable Use Policy

## Purpose

To define acceptable use of {{deployment-name}}, its administrative
interfaces, and {{organization}}'s information assets by staff and
contractors.

## Policy statements

1. Credentials and administrative tokens are personal, protected, and
   never shared.
2. Administrative access to {{deployment-name}} is used only for
   authorized operational purposes and is auditable.
3. Personal data in {{deployment-name}} is accessed only on a need basis
   and never copied to uncontrolled environments.
4. Security concerns and suspected incidents are reported promptly to
   {{security-contact}}.
5. Violations are handled under {{organization}}'s disciplinary process.

## Responsibilities

{{security-contact}} owns this policy. All staff acknowledge it at
onboarding and at annual refresh.

## Review

Reviewed at least annually.
