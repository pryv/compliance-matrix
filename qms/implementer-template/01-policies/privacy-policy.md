# {{organization}}: Privacy / Data Protection Policy

## Purpose

To state how {{organization}}, acting as {{controller-or-processor}} for
the personal data held in {{deployment-name}}, protects data subjects'
rights under the law of {{jurisdiction}}.

## Policy statements

1. **Lawful basis and purpose.** {{organization}} processes personal data
   only for stated, lawful purposes and records the basis. Consent, where
   relied upon, is captured and withdrawable.

2. **Data minimization.** {{deployment-name}} is configured to collect and
   expose only the data needed for its purpose, using Pryv's streams,
   accesses and permissions.

3. **Data-subject rights.** Requests for access, rectification, erasure,
   portability and restriction are handled through the data-subject-rights
   procedure within the statutory timeframe.

4. **Retention.** Data is retained per {{retention-policy}} and deleted
   when no longer needed.

5. **Subprocessors and transfers.** Hosting and any subprocessors are
   under contract; cross-border transfers rely on a documented basis.

6. **Breach handling.** Personal-data breaches are assessed and, where
   required, notified to the supervisory authority and affected subjects
   within the statutory deadline.

## Controller / processor responsibility split

{{organization}} uses the compliance matrix gap report for its scopes to
determine which obligations Pryv carries and which {{organization}} runs
itself. Those operator-owned obligations are implemented by the procedures
in this QMS.

## Responsibilities

{{dpo-contact}} owns this policy and coordinates data-protection impact
assessments.

## Review

Reviewed at least annually and when applicable law changes.
