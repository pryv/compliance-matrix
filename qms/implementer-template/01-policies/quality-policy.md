# {{organization}} — Quality Policy

## Purpose

{{organization}} commits to operating {{deployment-name}} reliably and in
conformance with applicable quality, security and data-protection
requirements in {{jurisdiction}}.

## Policy statements

1. {{organization}} operates {{deployment-name}} according to documented
   procedures and keeps records that evidence conformance.
2. Changes to the deployment are controlled and tested before they reach
   production.
3. Defects and incidents are tracked to resolution; recurring issues drive
   process improvement.
4. Staff are competent and aware of their quality, security and privacy
   obligations.
5. This QMS is reviewed by management at planned intervals.

## Objectives

{{organization}} tracks: service availability, incident count and
resolution time, backup success rate, and the status of data-subject
requests.

## Responsibilities

{{quality-owner}} owns this policy. All staff operate within the procedures
it governs.

## Review

Reviewed at least annually.
